捨てるものリスト (Sutemono List)

Support

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This English page is a reference translation of the Japanese original, provided for convenience. The Japanese text is the official version; where the two differ, the Japanese text applies.

An app that puts the things you are unsure about on hold with a deadline, and when the deadline comes asks you, “Did you use it?”.

FAQ

The deadline has come, but I didn’t get a notification

  1. Check that “通知を受け取る” (Receive notifications) is turned on in “設定” (Settings) inside the app.
  2. In the device’s Settings app, go to “捨てるものリスト” → “Notifications” and check that notifications are allowed. If this is off, notifications will not arrive even if the app-side setting is on.
  3. If the app’s home screen shows “通知がオフになっています” (Notifications are off), tapping that row opens the Settings app.

Even if a notification does not arrive, items whose deadline has passed remain under “期限が来ました” (Deadline reached) on the home screen. No records are lost.

What happens after I press “あとで答える” (Answer later)?

The item stays under “期限が来ました” (Deadline reached) on the home screen. Tap “答える” (Answer) when you are ready. The same notification will not be sent again.

How many times can I extend a deadline?

Once. If deadlines could be extended without limit, the holding area would turn into a mere storeroom. The number of extensions you have left is always shown on the item’s detail screen.

Where are photos stored? Are they sent anywhere?

They are stored only on this device. The app has no network functionality, so neither photos nor notes ever leave the device. When you decide whether to let go of an item or keep it, the photo is automatically shrunk to a size suitable for the record.

Will my data carry over if I switch devices?

It will if you restore from a device backup (such as an iCloud backup). The app has no sync feature of its own.

I made a purchase, but the limit hasn’t been removed

Try “設定” (Settings) → “購入を復元” (Restore purchases) inside the app. You need to be signed in with the same Apple ID that you used when you purchased.

What is free and what is paid?

Putting items on hold, and answering “used it” to keep them, are always unlimited and free. Letting go of an item and keeping it in the record is free for up to 10 items; beyond that, a one-time purchase is required to unlock it.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

It is deleted from the device. This applies to all photos, notes and records. Please retrieve anything you need before deleting.
